Real-Time Fraud Intelligence Platform

Streaming ML, graph analytics, MLOps and an LLM analyst copilot

The problem. A per-transaction fraud model caught 99% of card testing, but only 1.4% of coordinated fraud rings: every ring payment looks normal on its own.

What I built.

  • A Kafka event stream feeding stateful per-card features
  • A hybrid rules + Isolation Forest scorer handling 3,700+ events/sec into a DuckDB lakehouse
  • A card-device graph that finds rings
  • A shadow challenger model with graph features
  • PSI drift monitoring
  • A Claude copilot that writes evidence-cited case briefs using read-only tools

The result. Ring recall rose to 94.5% at the same analyst workload, and 14 of 15 rings were found with 100% precision.

Supply-Chain Digital Twin

Forecasting, network resilience, optimisation and Monte Carlo risk

The problem. A five-tier electronics supply chain needs to know which suppliers it cannot lose and what each disruption would cost.

What I built.

  • An Airflow ELT with a point-in-time feature store
  • Calibrated P10–P90 quantile demand forecasts
  • NetworkX analysis that finds single points of failure
  • SciPy safety-stock and network-flow optimisation
  • A Streamlit control tower that re-simulates the whole network for each scenario

The result. Forecast error was 40% lower than seasonal naive, and the analysis found 26 single points of failure. A Taiwan Strait blockade puts $43.4M at risk in 8 weeks.
